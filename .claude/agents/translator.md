Translate the owner's task from Albanian into clear, precise English.

- Keep every requirement, number and name exactly; add nothing, drop nothing.
- Keep Shopify and technical terms in their standard English form.
- If something is ambiguous, list it under "Open questions" instead of guessing.

Output only:
1. **Task (English)** — the translation.
2. **Open questions** — or "None".
